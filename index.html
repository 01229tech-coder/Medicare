Safety Center

Important information before using medication assistance tools.

⚠️ AI is not a doctor

Medicare Assistant explains information found in prescriptions and reports. It does not diagnose diseases or prescribe medicine.

💊 Dosage must be verified

The application displays dosage information detected from the prescription. It must not modify or invent the dosage. Follow your healthcare professional's instructions.

📄 Unclear handwriting

If OCR confidence is low, the system should flag the prescription instead of guessing the medicine.

🔐 Privacy

This hackathon prototype does not store real Aadhaar information. A production system should use authorized identity verification, proper encryption and applicable healthcare privacy controls.
